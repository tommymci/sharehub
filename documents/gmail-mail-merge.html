Gmail Mail Merge

Send a personalised email to a whole list. Each person gets their own private message. 10 minutes, no add-ons.

1. Make a Google Sheet

Row 1 = column headers. One column must hold the email addresses.

EmailFirst NameSchoolTopic
derek.chan@plkps.edu.hkDerekPLK Primary Schoolyour Workspace renewal
mary.wong@stpaul.edu.hkMarySt Paul's Collegemoving school mail to Gmail

No blank rows in the middle — Gmail stops reading there.

2. Turn on Mail merge

In Gmail on a computer: Compose → full-screen the window → click in the To field → switch on Mail merge at the right of that row.

│ To                    ( ●─) Mail merge   Cc  Bcc │  ← here
│ [ Send ▾ ]  ← "Schedule send", NOT the merge

Not there? Full-screen the window first. Then check you're sending from your own address, with no scheduled send and Confidential mode off. Still missing means your Workspace plan lacks it — ask IT.

3. Load your list

Click the Sheets icon in the To field → pick your Sheet → choose the Email column → Insert. Check the recipient count looks right.

4. Write it with @ tags

Type @ in the subject or body and pick a column. Example:

Subject: @First Name, a question about @School

Hi @First Name,

I work with Hong Kong schools on @Topic — worth a short chat with @School?

Derek gets: "Hi Derek, I work with Hong Kong schools on your Workspace renewal — worth a short chat with PLK Primary School?"

Click a tag to set a default (e.g. there) so empty cells don't send "Hi ,".

5. Test, then send

Add yourself as a row, re-load the list, and send. There's no preview and no undo. Check your copy, delete the test row, reload, send for real.

Limits

  • 1,500 recipients per day.
  • Text only — no per-person images, links or attachments.
  • No Cc, Bcc, or scheduled send.
  • Gmail adds an unsubscribe link you can't remove.

Need per-person images or links? That needs a script instead — ask Tommy.
