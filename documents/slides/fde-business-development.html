The decisions ahead

Everything before this is settled. These are not.

01

What do we sell?

The engagement itself, or the consumption behind it? The price, the contract and the headcount all follow from this one answer.

02

How do we price it?

A deployment fee, a share of the outcome, or enter at cost and trade that for the consumption that follows.

03

How are FDEs compensated?

Base salary alone, or base plus a share of what the engagement generates? An FDE drives the deployment fee and the consumption behind it — the model should reflect that.
